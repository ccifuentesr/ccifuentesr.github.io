01

RUNA

RUNA is a Python web application for stellar characterisation. It integrates catalogue data, quality indicators and published empirical relations in one interface. Its supervised-learning component estimates M-dwarf spectral subtype from Gaia and 2MASS photometry, with a quadratic baseline and a random-forest comparison evaluated on a separate test set.

02

KOBE stellar characterisation

Turns telescope spectra into estimates of a star's temperature and chemical composition. The Python workflow prepares the measurements, flags results that need review, and checks them against well-studied reference stars. Telescope data must be supplied separately.
