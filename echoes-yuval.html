Making Sense of a World in Crisis

“You said your next book might be your last. What is your purpose in life? Do you think it will change? And if so, how do you anticipate it will change?”

I don't think in terms of purposes. I try to understand life. And I think one of the most misleading things about life is that the human mind tends to think in stories, but reality is not a story. We constantly try to impose a story on reality and, of course, with ourselves as very important heroes at the center of the narrative. But reality doesn't work like that. So as long as we keep being attached to these ideas of what is the story of the universe, what is the meaning of life, what is the purpose of life, it guarantees that we misunderstand what life actually is.
