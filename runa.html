RUNA brings together catalogue identification, photometry, published empirical relations and quality indicators that I developed and used during my research. A selected star becomes the common input for the application, keeping each calculation traceable to its source data and method.

MACHINE-LEARNING BUILT-IN

SPECTRAL-TYPE ESTIMATE

See the star in a new light.

A quadratic G-J colour model estimates M-dwarf subtype from 1,509 selected stars across M0–M5.5.

Based on Cifuentes et al. (2020)

0.247subtypes mean absolute error on a separate test set

Unresolved companions may shift the combined G−J colour.

UNRESOLVED BINARIES DETECTION

More than meets the eye.

A random forest uses eight Gaia DR3 diagnostics to score close unresolved multiplicity.

Based on Cifuentes et al. (2025)

0.955ROC AUC · one system-separated test split

Internal evaluation only. The score is uncalibrated and does not confirm a binary or establish external performance.

FEATURES

01 · IDENTIFICATION

One selected star

SIMBAD name resolution. Gaia DR3 astrometry and radial velocity. Sloan sky views.

02 · PHOTOMETRY

Measurements across bands

Gaia colour–magnitude context, catalogue photometry, observed SED, approximate G−J distance and VOSA-compatible export.

03 · PARAMETERS

Published relations

Absolute magnitudes, published luminosities, mass estimates within calibration limits and experimental metallicity.

04 · MULTIPLICITY

Indicators and neighbours

Gaia multiplicity criteria, nearby-source inspection and RVS score versus RUWE. Indicators suggest possible multiplicity. They do not confirm it.

05 · KINEMATICS

Coordinates and groups

Propagate coordinates to an epoch and prepare inputs for BANYAN Σ young-association analysis.

06 · TIME SERIES & PLANETS

TESS and archive records

Available TESS light curves from MAST and NASA Exoplanet Archive searches by star name or catalogue ID.

07 · REFERENCE DATA

Published stellar values

Browse published average values from K5 V to L2.0 in Cifuentes et al. (2020), Table 6.
