03 / Spectroscopic binaries · Draft

Hidden companions

One point of light. More than one star.

I turn repeated stellar spectra into radial-velocity measurements, separate candidate components and test the evidence for orbital motion. The workflow connects spectral quality control, cross-correlation and SB1/SB2 modelling.

01 / Prepare

Keep the signal

Apply velocity corrections, mask telluric absorption and fit the continuum. Retain spectra with enough signal to measure the profile.

02 / Correlate

Many lines, one profile

Cross-correlate with an M5 mask, locate the minima and measure their velocities and widths.

03 / Compare

Follow the components

Compare profiles across epochs, distinguish single and double signals, and carry the usable velocities into the orbital analysis.
